Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take emergency calls at night and on weekends?

Yes. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 5 PM, and emergency service is available around the clock. Call (770) 534-2584 any time.

Do you work on both homes and industrial plants?

Yes. We handle homes and businesses as well as industrial plants, from a repair at home to control systems on a factory floor.

Do you inspect boat docks?

Boat dock inspections are part of our residential work, along with general electrical inspections and ground testing.

What are PLCs and VFDs?

A PLC is the programmable controller that runs a machine or process. A VFD is a variable frequency drive that controls a motor's speed. We program and troubleshoot both.

Do you offer maintenance programs?

Commercial and industrial customers can set up preventative or customized maintenance programs.

Is it safe to keep using a breaker that keeps tripping?

Not for long. A breaker trips to protect the wiring from overload or a fault. If it keeps happening, turn off what's on that circuit and have it checked.
